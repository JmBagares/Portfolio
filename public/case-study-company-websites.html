Project and Responsibilities

During my internship at Magnaval Services, I built and deployed two company websites. Magnaval Services needed a clear presentation of its services, while Warduz Petshop needed an organized presentation of its pet-shop content. My work covered page building, responsive layouts, content organization, and deployment.

What I Implemented

  • Page building: used WordPress and Elementor to create and customize the websites.
  • Responsive presentation: refined page layouts and service or product content for desktop and mobile visitors.
  • Deployment: published the websites using cPanel and custom domain configuration.
  • Client support: supported client requests and prepared technical documentation as part of the internship.
